01 / The model

Most physical therapy is three patients an hour.This one isn't.

You will not be left on a table with a heat pack while somebody else runs the room. From your first visit to your last, you work with the same licensed clinician, and your plan is built around what your body is actually doing, not printed off a sheet for your diagnosis.

  1. A proper first look

    How far the joint moves, how strong it is, how much it can handle, and how everything around it is working. Not just the spot that hurts.

  2. A plan on day one

    Your exercises start at the first visit, not three appointments later. You leave knowing what to do at home and why it helps.

  3. Hands-on treatment

    Massage, joint work and soft-tissue release to get the movement back. The exercises are what make it stay.

  4. Progress you can see

    Clear goals set on the first day, then checked again later. Progress is tracked and logged every session, so you are not guessing.

  5. Machines help. They don't do the work.

    Electrical stimulation, TENS and hot or cold packs all have their place. Their place is supporting the treatment, not replacing it.

Sport-specific rehabilitation

Get back to competing with confidence. Proper testing tells us when you are actually ready, and we train the rest of you while the injured part catches up, so you do not lose the fitness you worked for.

Rehab carries on until you can handle what your sport actually asks of you:

Going back to play is a decision based on test results, not on the calendar or on how long it has been. Ordinary therapy stops when the pain stops. This stops when you can prove the body part can take the load again.

04 / New at BodyForge

Professional recovery.Thirty minutes.

A weekend recovery session at BodyForge is thirty minutes of hands-on work run by the same clinical team that handles the rehab. Recovery with a clinical edge, not a spa. In, reset, and back to your day.

What a session is built from

Manual massage
Soft-tissue work through the muscle and fascia that has tightened up.
Assisted stretching
Someone else holds the position, so the stretch reaches range you cannot hold alone.
Joint mobilization
Graded pressure at the joint to restore glide where it has stopped moving fully.
Cupping
Suction that lifts the tissue rather than pressing into it.
Theragun
Percussive therapy, used to take the edge off a muscle quickly.
Normatec compression
Boots that inflate from the foot up to flush the legs after a heavy week.
